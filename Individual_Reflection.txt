Individual Reflection — ExceptionLab

I implemented FileOpenException and ParseException, including their constructors and what() methods. I implemented openFileForReading() to throw FileOpenException when the file cannot be opened. I implemented six overloaded geometry calculations for square, rectangle, and circle area and perimeter/circumference. Each function validates dimensions and throws std::invalid_argument when a dimension is zero or negative.

In main(), I added file-opening exception handling and per-record handling for ParseException, std::invalid_argument, and std::exception. The file is read one complete line at a time, so an unknown shape such as cuboid generates one parse error for that record rather than treating its numeric dimensions as additional shapes. Processing continues after each invalid record.

This is an individual implementation, so no teammate merge conflict or teammate review is claimed.
